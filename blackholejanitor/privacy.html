Black Hole Janitor – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 October 2026

Black Hole Janitor ("the game") is made by mfaru ("we"). This policy explains what information is processed when you play the game on iOS or Android.

Information we collect

We do not ask for your name, e-mail address or any account, and we do not run our own servers. Your game progress (coins, upgrades, unlocked zones, sound settings) is stored only on your device and is removed when you uninstall the game.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows optional rewarded video ads through Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google's Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process:

  • device identifiers such as the advertising ID (Android) or IDFA (iOS, only if you allow tracking),
  • approximate location derived from your IP address,
  • interactions with ads and diagnostic information (for example crash and performance data).

This information is processed by Google under its own policies: Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Your choices

  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: on first launch you are asked for consent through Google's consent form. You can change your choice at any time with the "i" button in the top-right corner of the game.
  • iOS: the game asks for permission to track only through the system App Tracking Transparency prompt. You can change it in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Settings > Google > Ads.
  • Ads are always optional: you choose whether to watch a reward video.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
